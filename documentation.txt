Phase 1: Colab Environment
What was implemented:

1. Google Drive Integration: Automatic mounting of Google Drive and the instantiation of the persistent project directory structure.

2. Environment & Hardware Telemetry: A diagnostic script to read Python, TensorFlow, and Keras versions, along with system RAM, disk space, and exact GPU memory metrics.

3. Reproducibility Anchors: Global random seed initialization across Python, NumPy, and TensorFlow to ensure behavior is as repeatable as possible on hardware.

Why it is required:
Google Colab limits your compute environment (often capping at ~12 hours of runtime, 15GB GPU VRAM on a T4, and transient local storage). If the instance disconnects, all local files vanish. By mounting Google Drive immediately and establishing our folders, we ensure artifacts survive. The telemetry script is vital because attempting to train a Transformer Base on a CPU or an overloaded GPU will instantly fail. Setting deterministic seeds minimizes the variance when debugging complex tensor operations.
